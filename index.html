Welcome to the Atlas Forge beta

Use this quick path for a complete takeoff test. Plans and markups remain in this browser unless you print or export them.

1. Open plansOpen one or several PDF, DXF, PNG, or JPG files. Use the tabs and page thumbnails to move between drawings.
2. CalibrateSelect Calibrate scale, click two known points, and enter the real distance before measuring.
3. Measure and markUse length, separate segments, area, angle, count, expansion joint, or freehand tools. Smart snap helps lock onto nearby points.
4. Check railingSelect a measured rail run, choose a product profile, and calculate pieces, posts, sleeves, joints, and corner bends.
PM beta checklist
  • Open a multipage PDF and at least two files at once.
  • Calibrate against a known dimension and verify a measurement.
  • Try separate segments, count, angle, and an expansion joint.
  • Run a railing calculation, then print or export the markup.
  • Report anything confusing, incorrect, slow, or missing.
